HarmonicBalance.jl

Mar 1, 2024 · 1 min read

Code
HarmonicBalance.jl
projects

HarmonicBalance.jl is a Julia package implementing multi-frequency Floquet expansions to find and analyse steady states of driven nonlinear (quantum) systems. I am a maintainer of the package since March 2024.

The textbook example is the Duffing oscillator. Harmonic balance turns its equation of motion into an algebraic condition on the response amplitude, and as the drive grows the resonance bends over into a region with two stable states and an unstable one between them.
